1. Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

VeriPass ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the VeriPass platform.

1.1 Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Username, email address (if provided), role (User/Producer/Logistics/Retailer), and passkey (hashed)
  • Wallet Data: Algorand wallet address (derived from mnemonic, never stored), wallet session identifiers
  • Product Data: QR codes scanned, product verification results, supply-chain checkpoints
  • Payment Data: Algorand TestNet transaction IDs, amounts, timestamps (all on-chain and publicly verifiable)
  • Usage Data: Number of verifications, AI agent queries, spending patterns

1.2 How We Use Your Information

  • To provide product verification and supply-chain passport services
  • To process x402 micropayments on Algorand TestNet
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • To power AI agent queries and generate verification reports
  • To improve platform performance and security

1.3 Data Storage & Security

Key Security Measures:
  • Mnemonics are sent over HTTPS once to derive wallet addresses, then immediately discarded — never stored
  • Passkeys are stored using secure hashing (scrypt)
  • All API endpoints require authentication via HMAC-signed tokens
  • Payment data lives on Algorand TestNet (public blockchain) — we store only transaction references

1.4 Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. Upon account termination (including wallet-based accounts), personal data is deleted. Blockchain transactions are permanent and public by nature of the Algorand network.

1.5 Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini AI: Used for product verification and AI agent queries
  • Algorand TestNet: Payment processing and transaction settlement
  • Pera Wallet: Optional wallet connection for authentication and payments
  • Google OAuth: Optional sign-in authentication

1.6 Your Rights

  • Access your account data at any time
  • Request account termination and data deletion
  • Export your payment history and verification records
  • Control your spending limits and notification preferences

2. Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026

By accessing or using VeriPass, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

2.1 Description of Service

VeriPass is an anti-counterfeit product-passport platform that uses:

  • x402 HTTP Payment Protocol for pay-per-use micropayments on Algorand TestNet
  • AI-powered agents for product verification, market analysis, and supply-chain intelligence
  • Blockchain-based payments using native Algorand ALGO transactions

2.2 Account Types

Role Description Access
User End consumer verifying products Scan, verify, AI chat, wallet
Producer Manufacturer creating supply-chain checkpoints User features + digital signatures
Logistics Shipping/transport provider User features + digital signatures
Retailer Store/seller verifying received goods User features + digital signatures

2.3 Payment Terms

  • All payments are in Algorand TestNet ALGO (not real currency)
  • Per-verification fee: 0.002 ALGO (~₹0.14 at TestNet rates)
  • AI agent queries: 0.003–0.005 ALGO per query
  • Payments are direct wallet-to-wallet transfers — no intermediaries
  • Users can set spending limits via the Wallet screen
  • All transactions are publicly verifiable on Lora Explorer

2.4 User Responsibilities

  • Maintain the security of your passkey and wallet mnemonic
  • Do not share your account credentials with others
  • Use the platform only for lawful purposes
  • Understand that product verification results are informational — always exercise independent judgment

2.5 Prohibited Activities

  • Attempting to circumvent x402 payment requirements
  • Uploading malicious QR codes or product data
  • Using the platform to facilitate counterfeit product distribution
  • Automated scraping or abuse of AI agent endpoints
